Fictional appointment records. Descriptive evidence for project review.

Executive view

The source contains 5,000 appointment records. Among 4,737 completed appointments, 51.2% were recorded as no-shows. The no-show rate rises from 26.6% for bookings 0-3 days ahead to 63.9% for bookings 31 or more days ahead. Treat these as descriptive patterns in synthetic data.

Interpretation and action

Review a low-cost confirmation and reminder workflow for long-lead appointments, then test it prospectively against current practice. Track attendance, cancellations, successful contact, refilled slots and staff workload. Keep the workflow supportive.

Appointment type, weekday and reminder-channel differences are descriptive. They do not support punitive treatment or demographic targeting.
